# Safety, evidence and truth boundaries

Project-E treats proposal quality as a chain of evidence decisions, not a tone setting.

## Evidence levels

Retrieved material is selected with provenance and metadata. A source can support a concrete project fact, provide limited adjacent evidence, or fail to support a claim. The writer receives only the selected evidence and the strategy's boundaries; it is not allowed to treat every retrieved sentence as permission to generalize.

## Critical warning propagation

An unresolved critical warning from proposal strategy remains present in the Stage 4 writing contract, the writer prompt and final validation. The independent auditor can add findings, but a clean auditor response cannot erase an upstream warning. This protects against a downstream model simply failing to rediscover an unsafe claim.

High-risk positioning labels such as **expert**, **senior**, **production**, **enterprise**, **real-time** and **24/7** are not inherently forbidden. They require evidence directly supporting that level. Otherwise the system must use narrower, supportable wording or retain a critical review requirement.

## Writer and auditor

Stage 4A writes a client-facing proposal from mandatory client/commercial obligations plus selectable writing priorities. Stage 4B audits independently. Deterministic validation then checks required content, safety findings, structure, word count and warning propagation. Human review remains recommended whenever a critical issue survives.

## Human boundary

Project-E can collect, analyze, retrieve, score, strategize, write, audit, validate and report. A person reviews the result, chooses Apply/Keep Reviewing/Skip and performs any external Upwork submission. This is a deliberate safety and accountability boundary.
